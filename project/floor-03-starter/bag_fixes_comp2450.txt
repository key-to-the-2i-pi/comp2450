--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  WHY THE BODIES SURVIVE UNTOUCHED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every algorithm in Floor 1 and Floor 2 uses exactly four things from its
container: .size(), operator[], .begin(), .end(). Bag<T> provides all four,
and -- this is the load-bearing detail --

      using iterator       = typename std::vector<T>::iterator;
      using const_iterator = typename std::vector<T>::const_iterator;

Bag's iterators ARE vector's iterators. They are not a new type. So:

  - for (const auto& m : bestiary)  works (range-for needs begin/end).
  - std::sort(bag.begin(), bag.end(), cmp)  works, and is still a real
    random-access sort -- not a slow fallback.
  - v.begin() + lo  works (pointer arithmetic on a random-access iterator).
  - std::vector<Item> left(v.begin() + lo, v.begin() + mid)  works: you can
    still build a std::vector out of a range of Bag iterators. This is what
    lets Sort.cpp's merge buffers stay as they are.
